Data, effects and limitations

The catalog is the source; this planner targets Classic 3.5, but the APIs do not certify a version for every record. Selections do not validate a playable build. Check requirements, armor slots and conflicts in game.

How values are handled

The indexes do not provide base character attributes. Weapon DPS and armor defense use formulas from compact-3503AAClassic-20260925 with their specific item_grades.var_holdable_* and var_wearable_* multipliers, following the AAEmu primary implementation. Defense and attributes are partial equipment contributions. Migration transfers up to 300 points, with a maximum removal of 150 per attribute. Perfect Lunasigil core stats are included. Synthesis effects are entered from your actual item tooltip; they are contributions, not inferred character totals. Physical temper scales physical defense and Melee/Ranged DPS; magic temper scales magic defense, Magic DPS and Healing Power. Static item effects, grade buffs, supported gem modifiers and eligible static set bonuses are included as contributions. Triggered, timed and unsupported effects are listed separately. Equipment Points uses database formulas for base equipment, lunagems and lunafrosts. Synthesis does not add Equipment Points. Derived skill damage is not calculated. General and damage-type Received Damage reductions add in percentage points, following the confirmed Classic rule. Description placeholders remain unresolved.

Armor is filtered by category and database slot_type_id when available. Items missing from the database use category only; check their body slot manually. Weapon hands and requirements still need in-game verification.
